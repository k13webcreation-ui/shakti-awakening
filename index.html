Hello, I'm Sirisha

Sirisha Deolal

Founder & Life Coach · Shakti Awakening

I am a deeply compassionate and purpose-driven leader whose work is rooted in my own powerful journey of transformation. Growing up, I often struggled to understand my emotions or find where I truly belonged. Feeling different and out of place, I experienced firsthand the weight of self-doubt and the challenge of navigating a world that didn't always feel accepting.

Rather than allowing those experiences to define me, I used them as a catalyst for growth. Through exposure to diverse life experiences, the structure of corporate environments, and my studies in psychology, I began to understand myself on a deeper level. Step by step, I overcame social barriers, rebuilt my confidence, and found the strength to pursue my goals with purpose and clarity.

"My mission is not only to guide individuals toward success, but to remind them that they are worthy, capable, and never alone in their journey."
About Us

Shakti Awakening Life Coaching Services

A modern transformational life coaching brand focused on building confidence, emotional clarity, mindset transformation and personal growth.

Our Approach

We provide structured online coaching services designed to help individuals reconnect with themselves, overcome limiting beliefs, and create aligned lives. Our coaching process involves interactive, guided activities within sessions that encourage self-reflection, build practical skills, and promote accountability.

Our Commitment

Clients are supported in actively applying what they learn, ensuring they take ownership of their growth and consistently put in the work required to become their best selves. We are committed to helping individuals not only achieve their goals, but also embrace their identity, trust their journey, and step into the most empowered version of themselves.

How It Works

Your Coaching Journey

A clear, structured process from first contact to transformation.

1

Get In Touch

Contact us through social media or email. We'll reach out to learn a little more about you and what you're looking for.

2

Pre-Questionnaire

A short questionnaire form is sent to you to complete — helping us understand your needs, goals, and where you are in your journey.

3

Introductory Intake Session

We schedule a free introductory session to dive deeper into your goals, challenges, and areas of focus — ensuring the right fit.

4

Choose Your Package

Based on the intake session, we recommend the most suitable coaching package and session plan for your unique needs.

5

Begin Your Transformation

Sign the coaching agreement, schedule your sessions, and begin the work. Throughout the journey, we keep detailed notes to monitor your progress.
